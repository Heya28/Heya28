## Projects

### [Trace-Driven Cache Simulator](https://github.com/Heya28/Cache-Simulator)
I wanted to see what caches actually do, so I built one. It simulates N-way set-associative caches with LRU and IBM's **ARC**, and sorts every miss into compulsory, capacity or conflict. I tested it on real Valgrind traces from 4 C programs, and ARC beat LRU by **2.45 points** on frequency-skewed access patterns.

`C++17` `Cache architecture` `Trace analysis`

### [Custom Unix Shell](https://github.com/Heya28/Custom-Unix-Shell-)
Yes, I wrote my own shell. No, it won't replace zsh. It handles `fork` / `exec` / `wait`, pipelines, background jobs, process groups and `SIGINT` routing, and it reaps zombie processes properly with `WNOHANG`.

`C` `POSIX` `Signals`

### [Credit Spread Analyzer](https://github.com/Heya28/credit-spread-analyzer) · [Live app](https://credit-spread-analyzer.streamlit.app/)
A pipeline that pulls 26 years of bond yield data from the FRED API, flags market stress with a rolling 60-day Z-score, and backtests a mean-reversion strategy (Sharpe, drawdown, win rate).

`Python` `pandas` `Plotly` `Streamlit`

## Experience

### Intern at DRDO, New Delhi (May-July 2026)
Implemented the **Maximin Expected Flatness (MEF)** transfer attack on CNN-based face verification and contributed it to [face-recognition-transfer-attacks](https://github.com/cssanchit/face-recognition-transfer-attacks/).

Across 4 attacker and 5 victim models, MEF hit a **38.89% breach rate**, **9.7 points** above the strongest baseline (SI-NI-FGSM at 29.17%).
